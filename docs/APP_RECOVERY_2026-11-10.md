# Interactive app recovery · November 10, 2026

Several earlier v0 projects have recovered interfaces and pinned sources. The work covers Cosmic Creative Playground, Light Wobble Explorer, Resonance Mandala, Living Donut, and a Sideways Slip index. Sideways Slip has three runnable demos and a source map for 53 original routes. Fifty remain in the recovery queue.
