## Purpose and immutable boundaries
This record synthesizes ten AI assessments of the *Open Research, Independent Falsification, and Interdisciplinary Team Formation* concept. The reviews are advisory and may share training, assumptions, or originating material. Their agreement is **not** independent replication. The original reviews must remain separately preserved with source attribution; this synthesis must never replace or be presented as an exact transcript of them.

**Founding invitation:** Independent researchers may test, reject, revise, or replace originating mechanisms. Investigator compensation must reward rigorous, reproducible work rather than favorable outcomes. A demonstrated failure, a superior conventional alternative, or a justified change in research direction can each be a successful milestone.

**Design constraints:** Preserve open-ended scientific discovery; forbid retrospective alteration of confirmatory endpoints; preserve access to negative results; keep model proposals separate from independent authority over consequential actions.

## Review synthesis: repeated concerns
1. **Testability:** Candidate domains are not yet claim-specific, parameterized predictions. Identify claims, test versions, competing baselines, measurement windows, and failure conditions before confirmatory experiments.
2. **Independence:** An independent steward, appointed with conflict disclosures and meaningful protection against arbitrary removal, should control investigator selection, milestone quality review, dispute resolution, and publication—not the originator alone.
3. **Access:** Public visibility does not imply open-source licensing or a fully reproducible research release. Freeze the relevant artifact, dependency set, test data, configuration, and provenance. Private and protected material must be explicitly scoped.
4. **Publication:** Negative, inconclusive, and replacement findings must be publishable. Any privacy or coordinated-vulnerability-disclosure delay must be narrow, predefined, time-limited, and independently adjudicated.
5. **Comparators:** Standard security and control mechanisms are not strawmen. Baselines must match the property under test. An ordinary reference stack may be the best result.
6. **Generalization:** Physics, biological prediction, computational control, and agent authorization are separate claims. Similar terminology is not a shared mechanism.
7. **Funding fit:** Independent falsification is a research method, not by itself a topic-specific NSF X-Labs platform mission. A future solicitation requires its own mission, deliverables, team and topic-fit evidence.
8. **Founder dependence:** Give investigators an artifact-first review path and a shared, timestamped record of material technical clarifications.
9. **Evidence custody:** Separate claim versions and evidence states from lifecycle events; preserve old negative findings after revisions.
10. **External evidence:** News, AI consensus, and external incidents may motivate experiments; none proves the architecture's effectiveness.

## Three research pathways
### A — Independent discovery (exploratory)
Teams choose questions, interdisciplinary collaborators, rival hypotheses, and alternative implementations. A discovery milestone can be a useful negative result, a superior replacement, a newly formulated testable claim, or a documented no-go. Exploration must be labeled as exploratory and may change direction.

### B — Registered experimental falsification (confirmatory)
For each selected claim, lock before the outcome is inspected: claim version; artifact hash; environment; task; threat/failure assumptions; baseline; primary endpoint; effect-size or error tolerance; stopping rule; exclusion criteria; and authorized test boundary. Publish any amendments without deleting the original protocol.

### C — Independent governance red-team
Test whether fund custody, access, researcher selection, endpoint freezing, adverse-result publication, adjudication, and evidence-ledger custody survive a hostile but authorized exercise. Governance must not be declared independent solely on the basis of its charter.

## Candidate workstreams — NOT validated, NOT pre-registered
**GATE-01 / Destination-side authority:** With explicitly defined revocation semantics and harmless synthetic markers, attempt unauthorized, expired, replayed, or altered consequential actions, comparing against a well-implemented conventional destination policy engine with signed short-lived credentials and idempotent execution. Count false approvals, false denials, availability, throughput, and overhead.

**EXIT-01 / Residual-effects closure:** Interrupt synthetic workflows at specified points, including partial and irreversible simulated effects. Compare with standard workflow cancellation, idempotency and compensation. False declaration of clean completion is a critical failure; never reaching completion also fails separately defined usefulness criteria.

**AUDIT-01 / Independent records:** Attempt alteration, omission, tail truncation and reordering under an independent expected event count/head anchor. Distinguish chain-internal consistency from externally witnessed completeness.

**OSC-01 / Oscillatory computation:** Ask independent dynamical-systems researchers whether there is a precise distinguishing claim and a competent fixed/adaptive oscillator or control benchmark; compare under matched budgets and distribution shifts. Never infer novelty from a familiar phenomenon or analogy.

**CUST-01 / Selective prediction:** Require an executable definition of the proposed phase/coherence metric for a fixed model and compare risk–coverage and latency against strong uncertainty/abstention baselines. Undefined metrics => NOT TESTED, not 'supported'.

**PHYS-01 / Proposed THz physical-data test:** A reviewer suggested held-out FePS3 spectra versus accepted physical fits. This is a feasibility question, not an available validated prediction or committed experiment.

**BIO-01 / Sequence-to-function:** No domain-general biological benefit is established; a specific algorithm, dataset, external baseline and prospective endpoint are prerequisites before testing a biological performance claim.

**INFER-01 / Pattern-to-causation and authority:** Probe spurious-correlation scenarios with independent controls; distinguish inaccurate causal inference from a separate failure of authorization. Causal inference is not itself authority.

Investigators may reject all these examples and propose better tests. No arbitrary quantitative margin in the AI reviews should be adopted without justification and preregistration.

## Proposed claim / protocol registry schema
Each claim record should contain:
- stable claim_id, immutable claim_version, textual statement and scope
- type: empirical mechanism | engineering requirement | normative governance commitment | cross-domain conjecture
- owner for receipt custody (not sole scientific adjudicator)
- artifact ref + commit/hash and license/access status
- existing evidence references and explicitly untested implications
- competing baseline and domain-specific comparison rationale
- protocol ID, timestamp, evaluator identity, primary outcome and failure rule
- evidence status: NOT_TESTED | SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE | REFUTED_IN_SCOPE | INCONCLUSIVE
- lifecycle: ACTIVE | REVISED | SUPERSEDED | RETIRED
- immutable predecessor links, disputes, corrections and publication URL

A claim's revision must not erase a refutation of its predecessor. A local test pass must not be promoted to external assurance. Normative authority cannot be established from engineering compliance alone.

## Proposed independence charter checklist
- Constitute an independent scientific/methods board; disclose relationships, commercial interests, and appointment/removal rules.
- Use neutral fiscal custody or enforceable ring-fenced milestone commitments; quality payments must be outcome-independent.
- Separate project IP rights from researchers' rights to report findings; agree on access, patent/license limits, security and privacy constraints in advance.
- Preserve registered results from publication vetoes; any narrowly tailored sensitive-disclosure review has a finite adjudicable deadline.
- Provide independent storage of frozen releases, pre-registrations, raw receipts, exclusions, and contradictory reports.
- Require written authorization and a stop authority for any action touching external systems, physical actuation, protected data or clinical contexts.
- Maintain a founder clarification log. Founder input is context, not final adjudication.
- Disclose commercial overlap and do not use unvalidated findings as marketing endorsement.

## Proposed review gates, not a launch authorization
**Gate 0 — Inventory:** Identify public/private artifacts, formal licenses, claims, known limitations, and all original AI-review source records.

**Gate 1 — Independence design:** Written steward authority, conflict and funding mechanisms, publication terms, research security boundaries, and independent evidence custody proposed for review.

**Gate 2 — Independent exploratory selection:** Investigators select the initial experiments or replacement hypotheses. They may choose no-go or new directions.

**Gate 3 — Confirmatory freeze:** Named methodologists accept specific artifact/protocol/baseline registrations before outcome evaluation.

**Gate 4 — Execution and adverse findings:** Produce independent receipts and reports; record supported-in-scope, refuted-in-scope, inconclusive or not-tested without erasure.

**Gate 5 — Direction decision:** Continue, redirect, replace, or retire a track based on evidence value and mission fit, not merely whether QOTE survives.

## Proposed evidence-to-product discovery pathway — November 18 clarification
**Status:** PROPOSED / NOT FUNDED / NO PRODUCT VALIDATED. This is an additive research-organization design, not a new scientific result or a claim of novelty. It does not modify the preregistered endpoints, claim statuses, enforcement behavior, or research gates above.

**Originator's contribution and limits.** Kayser Medical PLLC supplies an appropriately licensed and access-controlled inventory of existing research, mathematical formulations, source code, frozen artifacts, prior failures, intellectual-property context, and timestamped factual clarifications when requested. The originator does not select favorable outcomes, direct independent methods, approve conclusions, veto unfavorable publication, or require continued use of QOTE/Resona mechanisms. Materials are shared only under agreed IP, privacy, security, and research-access terms.

**Investigator autonomy.** Independent investigators select hypotheses, rivals, controls, methods, collaborators and appropriately authorized test environments. They may refute, repair, replace, or retire the originating approach. Competent conventional baselines receive equal treatment; a better conventional replacement or justified no-go can satisfy a research milestone. A qualified independent steward and adjudication process, still to be constituted, oversee conflicts, custody, milestone quality and disputes.

**Discovery-to-product translation.** Investigators may nominate candidate tools, platforms, reference implementations or applications arising from reproducible findings—including products the originator did not anticipate. They are not required to propose a product, and no mechanism or product is guaranteed to advance. A nomination should document: (1) exact claim/version and independent evidence, including failures; (2) comparison against existing alternatives; (3) reproducibility and remaining limitations; (4) potential user/problem and market need; (5) technical feasibility, risk, security/safety constraints and operational burden; (6) licensing, background versus new IP, publication rights and proposed ownership; and (7) a falsifiable next-stage product experiment with stop criteria.

**Separate decisions.** Scientific investigators own the evidence and conclusions; an independent scientific/methods review adjudicates methodological quality; an appropriately constituted product/translation review assesses feasibility, utility and development priority under disclosed conflicts. Product continuation is not a promotion of a scientific claim to universal validity. Development, licensing, commercial ownership, and external deployment require separate agreements and authorizations, with no commitment implied here.

**Three complementary attack domains (illustrative, not fixed appointments).** (i) Nonlinear dynamics and oscillator models: topology, controls, noise, preregistered thresholds and matched-budget alternatives. (ii) Distributed authorization: revocation/commit serialization, egress enforcement, partitions and crash-recovery against conventional controls. (iii) Agent and systems safety: adversarial instructions, forged or missing evidence, cross-agent coordination, safety hazards and governance failure. Named subject-matter experts may be approached later, but none has agreed to participate or been appointed.

**Positioning and novelty discipline.** Independent replication, red teaming, ARPA-style milestones and translational research have precedents. The proposed differentiator is their governance-coupled integration: founder-supplied artifacts; funded independent falsification or replacement; separately preserved negative evidence; and investigator-originated, evidence-gated product nominations. Its organizational distinctiveness, practical value and QOTE/Resona technical novelty remain hypotheses for comparison and evaluation, not established firsts.

**NSF X-Labs fit gate.** Independent falsification is a method, not a standalone X-Labs technical mission. A submission must separately demonstrate fit to the actual applicable topic, a credible technical breakthrough objective, team/recruitment and management capacity, fundable activities, measurable milestones, and compliant intellectual-property/commercialization terms. NSF personnel and proposal reviewers are not presumed to supply the proposed research teams.

**Working principle:** The research is the starting material. Investigators decide what survives. Product candidates must earn advancement through evidence, feasibility and need. An inconclusive or negative result remains a valid outcome.
